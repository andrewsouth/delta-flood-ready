Upstream camera Representative photo

This is a representative photo, not Delta. It comes from a real USGS camera on the North Fork Virgin River near Springdale (site 09405500), standing in for an upstream camera the Delta pilot would add.
Gage height7.46 ft

Why it's here. A camera at an upstream bridge, abutment, or reservoir outlet lets observers and the custodian see what's coming before it reaches town, and check the forecast against the river.

For Delta. No USGS camera watches the Sevier near Delta today. A camera at a key crossing or the DMAD outlet is a candidate pilot investment.

The model. In the 2023 flood season, Utah's Division of Emergency Management and LiveView Technologies put mobile cameras on more than a dozen creeks and rivers (shown at floods.utah.gov), and UDOT used them to watch vulnerable bridges. A Delta camera could follow the same approach: placed at a vulnerable crossing in a high-water year and shared through the community layer.

Photo and readings: USGS HIVIS and USGS Water Data (provisional). State network details are from 2023 news reports; check floods.utah.gov for current cameras.
