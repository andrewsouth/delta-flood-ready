Delta Flood Ready Toolkit

The library disaster corner

A standing space of about 12 by 16 feet in the Delta City Library. It works year-round as an education space and turns into the town's flood information point as the tiers rise. Nothing in it is expensive: a bulletin wall, a map table, a shelf, a rack, a tablet, and a few chairs.

1

"The Sevier Now" wallTier light, this week's brief in both languages, and a season chart of snowpack and river flow that staff update at each brief.

2

Map tableA laminated flood map of Delta under glass, with markers so families can find their home, their route out, and their meeting place.

3

Ready shelfA model go-bag, sandbag how-to cards, and a "documents to copy" checklist. Display only; the take-home versions are on the rack.

4

Weather radio demoA NOAA Weather Radio set up to show how alerts sound, with a sign about where to get one.

5

Take-home rackCopies of the current brief, family plan templates, alert sign-up cards, and Layer 2 training flyers, in English and Spanish.

6

Sign-up tabletText-list sign-up, the pulse survey, and Layer 2 volunteer interest. Library staff can help at the desk.

7

Plan-night seatingFolding chairs for monthly plan nights, Extension workshops, and Layer 2 training, facing the wall display.

8

Kids' nookPicture books on weather and floods and a "pack your own go-bag" activity, so families come together.
